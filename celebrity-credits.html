Questions & picture credits

500 different celebrities and cultural figures, each with three original clues: Easy, Medium and Hard. All 500 appear at every clue difficulty. Celebrity familiarity is a separate editorial guide and varies across countries and generations. Hard questions emphasize substantial but less obvious achievements, works, decisions and plot events rather than birthdays or unrelated trivia.

The seven groups cover actors, musicians, scientists, political leaders, thinkers and writers, literary and legendary figures, and sportspeople. The literary and legendary group includes characters from fiction, folklore and religious epics, identified in the context of their traditions; inclusion makes no claim about religious belief. Mythic, epic and ancient figures may use later artistic depictions. Images of fictional characters may show illustrations or screen portrayals.

Every game shuffles celebrities and answers, without repeating a celebrity within a game. All-familiarity games give familiar names greater selection weight; selecting a familiarity group narrows the pool. Questions use the linked biographical and work references. The full machine-readable bank is celebrity-data.json.

Images and source license metadata were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons on November 1, 2026. Each image keeps its own attribution and license, including share-alike requirements where applicable. Hard clues for household names reveal enlarged eye crops before the full portrait. The local picture sheets contain cropped or resized versions; individual works remain under their listed licenses.
