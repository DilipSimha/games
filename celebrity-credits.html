Questions & picture credits

90 questions, six categories, three difficulty levels. Each difficulty has five entries per category. Difficulty reflects expected familiarity and clue specificity; it is a guide for family play. Questions and answer choices are shuffled afresh on every play, without repeated questions within a game.

Questions are original learning summaries of the linked reference articles. Historical beliefs, fictional stories and real biographies are kept distinct. Fictional characters use illustrations or screen portrayals. Portraits of people from antiquity may be later imagined depictions. These images are clues, not documentary evidence of an ancient person's appearance.

Image files and license metadata were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons on November 1, 2026. Each image retains its own linked license; cropped or resized versions are collected in a local picture sheet. The individual works remain available under their listed licenses, including the share-alike terms where applicable. Public-domain and no-known-restrictions records are labeled as supplied by the source. GODL-India images credit their originating government source through the linked records.
