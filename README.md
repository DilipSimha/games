# The Curiosity Atlas

A family-friendly collection of geography, history and people quizzes.

Play at **https://dilipsimha.github.io/games/**.

- **Word Game:** unscramble 1,200 words of 3–7 letters, with fresh random letter arrangements on every appearance and one minute per word. Exact spelling, 10 available points per word, a 3-point meaning hint, greyed-out wrong guesses, and manual next-question controls. Learn each revealed word with its meaning and an example sentence.
- **Guess the Celebrity:** 620 different celebrities and cultural figures across 13 categories, each with Easy, Medium and Hard clues. Celebrity familiarity is a separate filter. On Hard, household names reveal only their eyes halfway through the timer; the full portrait appears after a guess or timeout.
- **World Explorer:** choose a continent and identify countries, then discover capitals, flags, population and leadership facts.
- **The 50 States:** explore U.S. statehood, capitals, admission stories, presidents, population and election information.
- **India Explorer:** identify states and union territories, with capitals, population and chief-minister facts.
- **U.S. Presidents:** guess presidents from timeline clues and midway portraits.
- **India's Prime Ministers:** guess prime ministers and discover the presidents who served alongside them.

The site works on phones, tablets and computers. Each game includes countdowns and answer reveals. Data dates, sources and image credits are linked inside the games.
